# Social Studies

A free curriculum of short lessons on Marxist theory and on the anti-colonial, Black liberation and socialist movements that put it into practice. It is written for newcomers and meant to bring them to a principled commitment to Marxism.

## Curriculum

**Curriculum**:
The whole body of lessons, open to browse in any order.
_Avoid_: Pipeline, course, path

**Subject**:
A single thinker, movement or foundational area, with the lessons that teach it. The curriculum has one level of subjects.
_Avoid_: Category, section, topic, tradition

**Lesson**:
One sitting of study: a short run of cards followed by a quiz. Every lesson is either a Reading or a Study.
_Avoid_: Module, unit, chapter

**Reading**:
A lesson centred on one work, its main work, taught on that work's own terms.
_Avoid_: Book lesson, summary

**Series**:
Consecutive Readings that together cover one work too long for a single lesson.
_Avoid_: Multi-part lesson, chapter

**Study**:
A lesson centred on a concept, figure or movement, drawing on several works.
_Avoid_: Overview, topic lesson

**Card**:
One screen of a lesson, short enough to read at a glance.
_Avoid_: Page, slide

**Quiz**:
The fixed set of multiple-choice questions that closes a lesson, each tied back to the card that answers it.
_Avoid_: Test, assessment

## Sources

**Work**:
A text by a thinker or movement (a book, pamphlet, essay, speech or program) that lessons teach and cite.
_Avoid_: Text, piece, writing

**Main work**:
The one work a Reading teaches. It must be free to read online.
_Avoid_: Primary text, core text

**Source**:
A work or other reference that a lesson cites by name.
_Avoid_: Reference, citation (a citation is the marker on a card, not the thing cited)

**Interlocutor**:
A thinker outside the curriculum's line (a Trotskyist, anarchist, Western Marxist or social democrat) who appears only as a party to a debate inside another lesson, never as a subject.
_Avoid_: Critic, opponent

## People

**Reader**:
A person using the app. The curriculum assumes a newcomer with no prior theory.
_Avoid_: User, student, learner
